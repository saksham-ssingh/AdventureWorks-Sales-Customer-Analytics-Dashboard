# Project Overview

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard using the AdventureWorks dataset to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, and regional trends.
The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor key business KPIs, identify sales opportunities, analyze customer insights, and support data-driven decision making.

# Business Problem

Businesses often struggle to:

1. Track sales performance across regions
2. Identify top-performing and underperforming products
3. Understand customer purchasing behavior
4. Monitor return rates
5. Analyze revenue trends over time
6. Compare performance across categories and locations
   
This dashboard was developed to provide a centralized analytical solution for business monitoring and strategic decision making.

# Objectives

1. Analyze overall sales performance
2. Monitor key revenue KPIs
3. Identify high-performing products
4. Evaluate customer purchasing trends
5. Analyze regional sales distribution
6. Track return and order metrics
7. Enable interactive drill-through analysis

# Dashboard Features

1. Executive Dashboard

Provides a high-level overview of:

  a. Revenue KPIs
  b. Orders and returns
  c. Monthly sales trends
  d. Category-wise performance
  e. Overall business growth

Business Value: Helps executives and management teams quickly monitor business performance and make strategic decisions.

2. Product Detail Dashboard

Analyzes:

   a. Product-level performance
   b. Sales contribution
   c. Product trends
   d. KPI comparisons
   e. Target achievement

Business Value: Helps product and sales teams identify top-performing products and optimize inventory and sales strategies.

3. Customer Detail Dashboard

Provides insights into:

a.Customer purchasing behavior
b. Revenue contribution by customers
c. Order patterns
d. Customer segmentation
e. Customer-level performance trends

Business Value: Supports customer retention strategies and helps businesses identify valuable customer segments.

4. Geographic/Map Dashboard

Visualizes:

a. Regional sales performance
b. Geographic distribution of revenue
c. Location-based sales insights

Business Value: Helps management identify strong and weak performing regions for market expansion and operational planning.
